# Planning Holiday Ear Piercing in Williamsburg, VA

Winter gatherings are a common time for families to ask about a first ear piercing. The useful question is not how fast the visit can be squeezed in. It is whether the appointment, the starter earrings, and the first days of aftercare fit the child and the calendar.

[Blomdahl ear piercing in Williamsburg](/procedures/blomdahl-ear-piercing) is an appointment at Williamsburg Med Spa, 3900 Powhatan Parkway, Williamsburg, VA 23188. Jenny Coleman, MSN, RN, CPNP, PMHS, uses a sterile single-use cassette and hypoallergenic Medical Plastic or Medical Grade Titanium starter earrings. The visit is $45 for one ear or $80 for both ears.

## Who the visit is for

The same rules apply in November and December as the rest of the year. [Infant and kids ear piercing](/procedures/blomdahl-ear-piercing/for/children) is for parents planning a baby's or child's first earrings. Jenny generally pierces babies at 6 months or older, with pediatrician guidance, and she will not pierce if she has a safety concern.

Older children, teens, and adults can book the same hub. Sensitive ears and nickel questions belong on the [sensitive ears page](/procedures/blomdahl-ear-piercing/for/sensitive-ears). A closed hole should be reviewed at the clinic. Do not force jewelry through it at home.

## Leave room for aftercare

Starter earrings stay in through the early healing window. Cleaning, sports, swimming, and signs that need a call are covered in the [Williamsburg Blomdahl aftercare guide](/blog/ear-piercing-aftercare-williamsburg-va). If a holiday dinner, travel, or a sport is the day of the piercing, move the appointment. The clinic does not publish a holiday-week capacity number, and this page is not a limited-seat offer.

Published hours are Mon–Fri 6–8pm and Sat–Sun 10am–6pm. Call (804) 738-9483 or [book from the ear piercing hub](/consult?procedure=blomdahl-ear-piercing&utm_source=website&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=holiday_ear_piercing) and say who the visit is for.
