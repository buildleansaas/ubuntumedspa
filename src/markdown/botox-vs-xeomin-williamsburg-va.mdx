## What Botox and Xeomin have in common

Both Botox and Xeomin are prescription injectable treatments derived from botulinum toxin type A. In cosmetic care, both are used to temporarily reduce activity in selected muscles. That can soften lines that form from repeated expression, including frown lines, forehead lines, and crow's feet.

Both are done in the office, and your injector should go over aftercare, timing, contraindications, and possible side effects before treatment. If you are still deciding whether you want a wrinkle relaxer at all, start with the [Xeomin service page](/procedures/xeomin). If the concern is excessive sweating instead of expression lines, start with [hyperhidrosis treatment in Williamsburg](/procedures/hyperhidrosis-treatment).

## The differences patients ask about most

### 1) Formulation and manufacturing are different

Botox and Xeomin are distinct products. They belong to the same class, but neither one is mixed, dosed, or read as a stand-in for the other.

If you have had Botox elsewhere, that history helps. Your Xeomin plan is still built around the areas being treated, the way your muscles move, and the result you want.

### 2) Units are not interchangeable

Units are specific to each product and are not interchangeable between them.

When you compare quotes, compare the whole treatment plan rather than the price per unit.

### 3) Response can vary by person and area

Two patients can receive similar treatment plans and still notice different timing, strength of effect, or longevity. Muscle strength, baseline movement, prior treatment history, and treatment interval can all matter.

## Which one lasts longer?

It is reasonable to think of Botox and Xeomin as temporary treatments that often last in a similar range. For many people, visible smoothing lasts around 3 to 4 months, but this is not guaranteed and can vary by area, dose strategy, and individual response.

If how long it lasts is your main concern, tell Jenny early. Keeping to a steady maintenance plan usually helps more than switching products often without a clear reason.

## Which one looks more natural?

A natural look depends mostly on how the injections are planned.

A balanced result usually comes from:

- precise muscle mapping before injection,
- dose balance across adjacent muscle groups,
- clear communication about how much movement you want to keep.

If you want to look rested and still move your face, tell Jenny that in plain words at your consultation. It shapes the plan more than any brand preference.

## Safety and who is a good fit

Botox and Xeomin are both medical treatments that require appropriate screening.

Your consultation should cover:

- current medications and relevant health conditions,
- prior treatment response,
- treatment goals and risk tolerance,
- realistic timelines and maintenance planning.

As with any injectable, temporary side effects can include redness, bruising, tenderness, or swelling at injection sites. Rare but serious risks should be discussed directly with your provider before treatment.

## Questions to ask at your consultation

Bring this list with you:

1. What specific areas are we treating now?
2. What degree of movement do I want to preserve?
3. How did I respond to prior neuromodulator treatments, including if I have had Botox elsewhere?
4. What timing do I need for an event or photo deadline?
5. What maintenance schedule is realistic for me?

Once you have those five answers, deciding whether Xeomin fits is usually simple.
